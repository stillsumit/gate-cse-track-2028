Multi-Provider AI Setup (BYOK)

Connect a free OpenRouter key (sk-or-v1-...) or Google Gemini key (AIzaSy...)—or save both for automatic cross-provider failover.

1. Connect & Verify Your API Key(s)

Not Configured
Keys are stored 100% locally in your browser's localStorage and are never sent to any third-party server.

2. Option A: OpenRouter Free Key (Recommended)

Smart Router
  1. Open OpenRouter Keys: Visit openrouter.ai/settings/keys ↗ and sign in with Google or GitHub (no credit card required).
  2. Create Key: Click "Create Key" and copy the string starting with sk-or-v1-....
  3. Why OpenRouter? Uses openrouter/free + vision models (gemma-4, nemotron-3.5-vl) to route around single-server congestion automatically.

3. Option B: Google Gemini Free Key

Backup / Primary
  1. Open Google AI Studio: Visit aistudio.google.com/apikey ↗ and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Create Key: Click "Create API key" and copy the string starting with AIzaSy....
  3. Best Workflow: Use Win + Shift + S then Ctrl + V anywhere in GATE.TRACK to paste question clips instantly without saving files.
